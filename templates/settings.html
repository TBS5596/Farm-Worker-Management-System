Worker Identity Cards
Adds a third check at the capture point. The card is something the worker has, the PIN something they know, the face something they are. A worker without a card can still clock in with their worker number, and the audit log records when the card was not used.
Leave this on to keep all three checks. Turning it off makes the queue faster but drops to two: the card and the face.
{# The trade-off is stated here rather than left to the manual, because this is the screen on which it is actually decided. #}
This choice has a privacy consequence.
  • The NRC itself — anyone who finds a dropped card and scans it with a free phone app reads that worker's national registration number.
  • A one-way scramble of the NRC — still unique to the person and still derived from their NRC, but the NRC cannot be read back out of it. A lost card cannot be given a different number.
  • A generated card number — means nothing outside this system, and is the only option under which a lost card can be voided and replaced cleanly.
Changing this changes how new cards print. Cards already in workers' pockets keep working — the value on them has not changed, only the way it is drawn.
