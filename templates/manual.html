System Manual

Day-to-day operations: enrolling workers, verified clock-in, attendance, payroll, cameras and troubleshooting.

The one thing to understand first

A PIN on its own does not record attendance. When a worker clocks in, the camera compares the face in front of it with the face samples enrolled for that Worker ID. No match, no record. That is why enrolment is the setup step that matters most - a worker with no enrolled samples cannot clock in at all.

1) Setup Checklist, In Order
  1. Sign in as admin / admin and set a real password when asked.
  2. Settings - organisation name, farm coordinates, geofence radius, hourly rate, NAPSA and NHIMA rates, shift times, and the pay cycle (weekly, fortnightly, twice a month or monthly).
  3. CCTV - add the clock-in camera, press Test, then the button to make it the attendance camera.
  4. Workers - add each worker with an hourly rate and a unique PIN.
  5. Biometric - enrol at least three face samples per worker.
  6. Have a worker clock in from the home page and check Attendance.
  7. Payroll - pick the cycle and a date in the period, and generate.
  8. Tell workers they can check their own hours and payslips at /me on their phones (section 7).
2) Worker Onboarding

Example: add a new worker named "Moses Banda".

  1. Go to Workers and click Add Worker.
  2. Enter name, phone, NRC, department and hourly rate; set status to active.
  3. Leave Pay Cycle on Farm default unless this worker is paid on a different cycle from the rest of the farm.
  4. Create a unique PIN (at least 4 digits).
  5. Click Save Worker. A 4-digit Worker ID is generated automatically.
  6. Go to Biometric and enrol his face before his first shift.

3) Face Enrolment

On the Biometric page each worker has a row showing how many samples are stored. Ask the worker to stand square to the camera in even light, with no hat or sunglasses, then press the green camera button on their row. Repeat three to five times with small changes of angle and expression.

  • 3 / 8 green - enrolled and reliable.
  • 1 / 8 amber - enrolled but too few samples; matching will be unreliable.
  • Not enrolled - cannot clock in.

No camera to hand? Use the button to enrol from clear front-facing photographs instead. The button deletes all samples for that worker so you can start again.

Workers use the home page - they never sign in to the dashboard. They enter their Worker ID and PIN, choose Clock In or Clock Out, and look at the camera.

5) Attendance Records
  • Identity - a green badge with the match score means the face matched the enrolled template.
  • Location - "On site", or the distance if outside the radius.
  • Clip - opens the short video recorded at that punch.
  • Rebuild Daily Summaries - recalculates hours, overtime and lateness for the last 30 days.
  • Export CSV - the whole table for a spreadsheet or report.
6) Payroll

Example: pay the week ending Sunday 23 August.

  1. Go to Payroll. Leave Pay cycle on the farm default unless you are paying a different cycle.
  2. Set Date in the period to that Sunday.
  3. Click Generate Payroll.
  4. Check the rows, then export the CSV as the payment list.
  5. After paying, edit each row and set status to paid with the payment date. Marking a period paid is also what releases the payslip to the worker's phone.
Choosing the period

The farm can be paid weekly, fortnightly, twice a month or monthly, set on the Settings page. An individual worker can be put on a different cycle from their record on the Workers page - casual labour weekly and permanent staff monthly is a common arrangement.

CycleWhat the date you choose means
WeeklyThe last day of the week you are paying
FortnightlyThe last day of the fortnight
Twice a monthAny day in the half-month. It fills in 1-15, or 16 to month end
MonthlyAny day in the month. It fills in the whole month

Only workers on the cycle you chose are paid. Running the monthly period does not touch the weekly casuals, and the message afterwards says how many were left alone for that reason.

Hours come from the recorded sessions; gross pay, NAPSA, NHIMA and net pay are calculated from the worker's rate. Periods already marked paid are never overwritten, so re-running is safe. A hand-entered correction still has its pay calculated - you enter hours and a rate, never an amount.

If you see a red "already paid" message

A worker is skipped when a different paid period already covers some of these days. That happens when somebody moves from weekly to monthly: their first four weeks are already settled. Nothing went wrong - the system stopped a double payment. Either mark the old period unpaid, or generate from the date after it ends.

7) For Workers: Your Own Hours and Payslips

Workers can look up their own records without asking anyone. This is separate from clocking in, and it gives no access to the office pages.

Example: Musonda checks last month's payslip on his phone.

  1. On the farm WiFi, open the clock-in screen and tap My hours & payslips at the bottom (or go to /me).
  2. Type Worker ID 0001 and his PIN.
  3. Tap Take a photo and take a photo of his face.
  4. Tap Sign in.

Signing in takes three things: the Worker ID, the PIN, and a photo taken there and then.

Why the photo

Anyone who watched a worker type their PIN at the terminal still cannot open their payslips, because the system checks the face against the one enrolled for that Worker ID. A PIN was only ever meant to guard a clock-in; it is four digits, and it gets typed in front of a queue. A worker whose face is not enrolled cannot sign in here at all - enrol them on the Biometric page first.

TabWhat it shows
Home Whether they are clocked in right now, hours and days this week and this month, their own details, their pay cycle, and their last payslip
Attendance Every shift recorded for them, newest first, with the photo taken at clock-in
Payslips Each paid period, with hours, overtime, rate, gross pay, NAPSA, NHIMA and net pay

Everything here is read-only. A worker cannot see any other worker, cannot reach any office page, and cannot change a single figure. Corrections are made by a supervisor on the dashboard.

Why the current period is missing. A period appears only once it has been marked paid. Until then the figure can still change when payroll is regenerated, and showing a number that later moves causes more disputes than it settles.

If somebody is locked out. Five wrong attempts in fifteen minutes locks that Worker ID for fifteen minutes. Waiting clears it, and so does a supervisor resetting the PIN on the Workers page.

Settings has two controls for this: one turns the portal off entirely, and one drops sign-in to ID and PIN without the face check. Leave the face check on - it is what makes a PIN safe to use for wage history.

8) Cameras and Clips
  • Health Check opens every registered camera and records the result under Hardware Health. Worth running each morning.
  • Record 10s Clip records from the attendance camera on demand.
  • Clips are recorded around each punch instead of continuously, which keeps a small disk from filling while still giving a supervisor something to review.
  • The button chooses which camera does verification and snapshots; the rest are surveillance.

10) Edit and Reset Operations
  1. Click the row's button.
  2. The modal loads the existing values.
  3. Change what you need and submit.
  4. For a worker PIN or a user password, use the key icon instead.
11) Troubleshooting
SymptomCause and fix
Page will not open Check the address uses port 8010. Browsers block port 6000 outright
Blank camera feed Wrong source, another app holding the camera, or the USB device is not passed into Docker
Everyone rejected at clock-in Camera not working, or nobody is enrolled. Check the Biometric page
One genuine worker keeps failing Too few samples or poor light. Enrol more samples in the conditions they actually use
"Recognizer: correlation-fallback" warning opencv-contrib-python missing, or plain opencv-python installed alongside it. Reinstall from requirements.txt
Payroll generates nothing No recorded hours that week, or rebuild the daily summaries first
Payroll skipped a worker, "already paid" in red A different paid period already covers some of those days - usually after moving somebody from weekly to monthly. Nothing is broken; the system stopped a double payment. Mark the old period unpaid, or generate from the date after it ends.
Payroll skipped workers "on a different pay cycle" Correct behaviour. Run that cycle separately from the Pay cycle box.
Worker cannot sign in at /me Their face is not enrolled, or they have had five failed attempts in fifteen minutes. Enrol them on the Biometric page, or wait, or reset the PIN on Workers.
Worker sees no payslips Only paid periods appear. Mark the period paid on the Payroll page and it shows up.
"Take a photo" does nothing on a phone Allow the browser access to the camera. The button opens the phone's own camera app, so it works over plain http on the farm network.
Everyone signed out after a restart Set FMS_SECRET_KEY so the session key is stable
12) Routine
  • Each morning: CCTV → Health Check; confirm the attendance camera is online.
  • During the day: watch Attendance; refused attempts and their reasons appear on the Biometric page.
  • End of each pay period - Friday on a weekly cycle, month end on a monthly one: Payroll → choose the cycle and a date → generate → export → mark paid. Marking paid also releases the payslip to the worker's phone. If the farm runs two cycles, run each one separately.
  • New worker: add on Workers, then enrol at least three samples. Without enrolment they can neither clock in nor use the worker portal.
  • Worker leaves: deactivate on Workers. Records stay for the audit trail; they can no longer clock in.
